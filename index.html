    Look inside Trove's voxel models

    Open a folder of .blueprint files, or drop some here, and turn them around in 3D. Switch to Edit to recolour a model, change its materials or build onto it, then download it again. Files are read in your browser and never uploaded.

    Where are Trove's blueprints?

    Extract Trove's game archives with a modding tool and open the blueprints folder. Your browser may ask whether to "upload" the folder. That is its standard wording for letting a page read files; nothing leaves your computer.
